# Café media credits

These local files support a private Kirby fan project. Kirby and Kirby Café are © Nintendo / HAL Laboratory, Inc.

## Visuals

- `kirby-cafe-logo.webp` is the Kirby Café header logo from the [official Kirby Café site](https://kirbycafe.jp/cafe/).
- `kirby-waddle-dee-cafe.webp` is the Kirby, Waddle Dee, and Whispy Woods café illustration from the [official Kirby Café site](https://kirbycafe.jp/cafe/).
- `dream-land-motif.webp` is the reusable Dream Land footer motif from the [official Kirby Café site](https://kirbycafe.jp/cafe/).

The source WebP files were already compact and retain their original transparency.

## Music

Album names, credits, and track ordering are documented by the [official HAL Laboratory Kirby Café soundtrack page](https://www.hallab.co.jp/products/kirbycafecd/).

- `fresh-green-cafe-terrace.ogg` contains **Fresh Green Café Terrace / Green Greens**, arranged by Hirokazu Ando. Source album page at [KHInsider](https://downloads.khinsider.com/game-soundtracks/album/the-sound-of-kirby-cafe).
- `tomorrows-tea-times.ogg` contains **Tomorrow's Tea Times**, arranged by Hirokazu Ando. Source album page at [KHInsider](https://downloads.khinsider.com/game-soundtracks/album/the-sound-of-kirby-cafe-2).
- `green-warmth.ogg` contains **GREEN WARMTH**, arranged by Yuuta Ogasawara. Source album page at [KHInsider](https://downloads.khinsider.com/game-soundtracks/album/the-sound-of-kirby-cafe-2).

The local copies use stereo Opus audio in `.ogg` containers with a 40 kbps target bitrate for browser playback. These files remain copyrighted soundtrack material and are intended only for this private project.
